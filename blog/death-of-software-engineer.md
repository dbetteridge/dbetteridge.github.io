# The death of the software engineer has been greatly overstated

Every other thought leadership post I've come across for the last few years has been swinging wildly from one extreme to the other. 

Either engineering and white collar work is dead, replaced by "AI" and never to be done by humans again or "AI" is useless, hopeless and solved none of the authors problems. 

Having worked with, tested, built on and prodded at the internals over the last few years I'm inclined to say the truth as usual lies somewhere in the middle. 

The role of taking a well-specified ticket and turning it into code may become less valuable as code becomes cheaper to produce. But that is only one part of engineering. Understanding the problem, making trade-offs, shaping a product, and deciding what shouldn’t be built remain difficult. If anything, those responsibilities matter more when implementation can happen faster.

An agent can generate a solution that is plausible in isolation while missing the conventions, constraints, and intent of the wider product. Give agents little direction across enough tasks, and you may end up with a codebase that works in pieces but lacks coherence. It becomes harder for people to maintain and harder for the team to improve.

That's where you come in, your role is to apply critique to question the machine to put your foot down on codebase rules and patterns that are there for a good reason, to supply your own tastes to the overall whole. 

This includes applying good software engineering fundamentals, planning before building and breaking problems down in a way that is both easier to build and maintain but also easier for humans to reason about in concert with the machine. 

When producing code becomes cheaper, the bottlenecks move into product decisions, reviewing the code and integration into the wider product whole.

Human understanding is a design constraint. If you cannot explain a system , you don't understand the system and if the agents built it and your team cannot explain it then you've traded short-term velocity increases for a long-term inability to maintain or evolve it.

Go forth, be opinionated. Provide direction and be accountable for the results
